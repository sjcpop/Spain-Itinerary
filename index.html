How to use this page

Every place name is a link — tap it and it opens as a pin in Google Maps. At the bottom of each day there’s a Walk this day button that loads the whole day as one multi-stop route.

Colour tells you what’s settled: BOOKED is confirmed, TO BOOK still needs doing, OPTION is a choice, not a plan.

Poppy’s drop-offs and pick-ups sit in orange bands so you can’t scroll past them. The Listen before you go drawer on each day has podcast and video links for the car.
